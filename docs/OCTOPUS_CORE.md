# 🐙 OCTOPUS CORE

## Vision

Octopus is a Smart Business Operating System.

It is designed to work with different business types without changing the system core.

The system should be flexible, scalable and easy to use.

---

# Business Types

Examples

- Pet Shop
- Barber Shop
- Restaurant
- Super Market
- Dairy Shop
- Pharmacy
- Workshop
- Electronics Store
- Clothing Store

Business Type changes the user experience only.

It never limits Products or Services.

---

# Item Types

Every item is one of:

## Product

Examples

- Dog Food
- Shampoo
- Water Bottle
- Hair Dryer

## Service

Examples

- Haircut
- Grooming
- Oil Change
- Delivery

A business can contain both Products and Services.

---

# Sale Methods

Every item can support one or more sale methods.

Supported methods:

- Piece
- Weight
- Money
- Box
- Carton
- Meter
- Hour
- Day
- Package

Examples

Dog Food

✔ Weight

✔ Money

Water

✔ Piece

✔ Carton

Haircut

✔ Service

# Core Rules

Business Type changes the experience only.

Products and Services can exist together.

Sale Methods belong to the Item.

Units belong to the Item.

Permissions belong to Roles.

The system must always be scalable.

Never build for one business only.

Always build for every business.
